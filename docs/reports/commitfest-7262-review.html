Patch summary

The patch adds a --invalid-databases=skip|error option to pg_upgrade. Invalid databases (whose DROP DATABASE was interrupted, leaving datconnlimit=-2) are skipped by default and reported with a warning; with --invalid-databases=error they abort the upgrade (preserving the historical behavior). The get_db_infos query now filters AND d.datconnlimit <> -2 so invalid DBs are excluded from the dbarr list — they are neither dumped, restored, nor file-transferred. check_for_connection_status gains a new else if SKIP branch (before the catch-all abort branch) that logs a per-DB warning and sets skipped_invalid; the final check_ok() is suppressed when invalid DBs were skipped. The TAP test 002_pg_upgrade.pl adds --invalid-databases=error (abort) and default-skip (--check) sub-tests. Docs, typedefs.list, and option parsing are updated.
